[h1]Bush War 1965–1994[/h1]
Bush War brings the conflicts of southern Africa to Armored Brigade II, with a focus on the fighting in Angola and the Rhodesian Bush War.

I've aimed to keep the equipment, formations and dates grounded in historical research. I have done my best to recreate these military forces while fitting AB2's style of organisation. Don't expect these factions to perform well against the vanilla factions.

South Africa also includes some experimental SADF vehicles, such as the TTD and Rooikat 105 (Wargame: Red Dragon classics). I also gave Rhodesia T-55's based on the tests they did towards the end of 1979.

There are no missions or maps, just factions. I wanted to recreate some of the more famous battles, such as Cuito Cuanavale, but AB2 does not allow custom map assets.

There may be some issues with unit models, as they are not 100% complete.

Let me know in the comments about any issues or suggestions for historical accuracy.
